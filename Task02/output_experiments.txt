Aliasing, shallow copy and deep copy of a = [1, [2, 3]]
  alias b = a       after a[0] = 100 and a[1].append(99): copy is [100, [2, 3, 99]]
  copy.copy(a)      after a[0] = 100 and a[1].append(99): copy is [1, [2, 3, 99]]
  a[:]              after a[0] = 100 and a[1].append(99): copy is [1, [2, 3, 99]]
  copy.deepcopy(a)  after a[0] = 100 and a[1].append(99): copy is [1, [2, 3]]
  shallow copies copy the outer list only, so the inner list is still shared; deepcopy copies everything
